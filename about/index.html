About me

I’m an electrical and electronics engineering graduate from the University of Johannesburg. My work brings together software, machine learning, and physical systems.

For my final-year project, I built a robot that identifies and sorts recyclable waste. I’ve also worked on a hydrogen fuel-cell vehicle and built tools for vehicle price prediction and path planning.

I’m interested in applying machine learning to practical challenges across African industries, including agriculture and energy.
