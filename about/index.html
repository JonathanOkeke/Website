About me

I’m a software builder and senior analytics consultant based in Johannesburg.

At Business Science Corporation, I lead the design and delivery of agentic AI software for procurement. I build agents that can use tools, analyze data, and turn the results into recommendations that people can review and act on.

My earlier work in machine learning and data engineering shaped how I approach AI today. I’ve built systems for customer segmentation and churn prediction, and used experiments to measure whether those systems delivered value in practice. I bring that same focus on measurement to the agents I build now.

My Views on AI and What Comes Next

In a practical sense, I think a large part of software has been solved. Generative AI has made it much faster for me to turn an idea into working code, test it, and improve it. I use these tools every day, and I’m just as interested in what they make possible for whole organizations when they become part of how teams work.

I believe robotics is the next frontier. I’ve always been fascinated by what it takes for a machine to learn and act in the real world. I’m especially interested in the feedback loop that lets a robot try something, see where it went wrong, and use that experience to do better the next time. I spend a lot of my free time exploring how robots can build on what they learn and put it to use. One day, I’d like to help build robots that learn from their mistakes, get better with experience, and help us do more.
