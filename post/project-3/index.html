Hydrogen vehicle prototype

A single-seat hydrogen fuel-cell vehicle built by a four-person team for the Shell Eco-marathon.

Context
Shell Eco-marathon
Team result
African vehicle efficiency record
My role
Electronics, controller, and drivetrain

The challenge

Three teammates and I designed and built a prototype around a hydrogen fuel cell. The goal was to get a working vehicle onto the track and use as little energy as possible to complete the course.

My contribution

I designed the electronics schematics, programmed the Arduino that controlled the vehicle, and helped install and commission the drivetrain.

The drivetrain used a 48 V hub motor. A buck converter stepped the fuel cell’s output down to 5 V to power the Arduino and the other electronics.

On the track

We placed first, set an African Shell Eco-marathon vehicle efficiency record, and qualified for the global finals. Watching the vehicle complete its first lap was the moment all those late nights paid off.
